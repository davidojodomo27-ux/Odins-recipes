Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

It is baked in the oven until golden and bubbly on top.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Onion and garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles until soft
  2. Brown the ground beef with onion and garlic
  3. Add tomato sauce to the beef and simmer
  4. Layer noodles, meat sauce and cheese in a baking dish
  5. Repeat layers until dish is full
  6. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes
  7. Let cool for 10 minutes then serve
